# Product Manifest

## Product capability

Commissioning answers: "Who receives a commission, why, how much, under which plan/rule, and when is it ready for settlement?"

## Core workflows

1. Define commission plan.
2. Attach rates and rules.
3. Resolve attribution source.
4. Calculate commission for an economic event.
5. Write calculation and ledger entries.
6. Mark ledger entries as settlement-ready.
7. Export settlement basis to Paying/Payouting.

## User/business concepts

- Partner
- Affiliate
- Reseller
- Marketplace seller
- Referral source
- Commission plan
- Commission rate
- Commission rule
- Commission attribution
- Commission calculation
- Commission ledger entry
- Settlement batch
